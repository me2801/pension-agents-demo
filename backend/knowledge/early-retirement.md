# Early retirement (eerder met pensioen)

## When you can retire early
Members may retire up to 10 years before the state pension age (AOW age). You choose the start date yourself, as long as it is the first day of a month. You do not need to stop working completely: partial retirement is possible from 10% up to 100% of your pension.

## Effect on your pension
Retiring earlier means your pension is paid out for a longer period. Your yearly pension is therefore lower. As an indication, every year of early retirement lowers the yearly pension by about 6 to 7 percent. You also stop building up pension from the moment you retire.

## How to apply
Apply at least six months before your chosen start date through the member portal. You will receive a written offer with your exact amounts. The offer is valid for three months.
