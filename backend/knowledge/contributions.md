# Contributions

## Who pays
The pension contribution is paid by your employer, who deducts your share from your gross salary. The employer pays two thirds of the total contribution and the employee pays one third.

## Pensionable salary
Pension is built up over your pensionable salary: your gross annual salary including holiday pay, minus the franchise. The franchise is the part of your salary on which you do not build up pension, because you will receive a state pension (AOW) over it.

## Voluntary extra savings
Members can save extra for their pension through the voluntary savings plan. Extra contributions are deducted from your gross salary and are tax-free up to the legal maximum.
