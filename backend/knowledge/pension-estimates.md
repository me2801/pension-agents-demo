# Pension estimates

## Online pension planner
The online pension planner in the member portal shows a personal estimate of your future pension. You can see what happens if you retire earlier or later, work part-time, or exchange partner pension for a higher retirement pension. The planner uses your accrued pension and assumes you keep working under your current conditions.

## Written estimate
A written pension estimate can be requested free of charge once per year. It is sent by post within four weeks and contains your accrued pension, your expected pension at state pension age and the partner pension.

## Uniform pension overview
Every year in the spring you receive the uniform pension overview (UPO). It shows the pension you have accrued so far and the pension you can expect if you keep working until state pension age.
