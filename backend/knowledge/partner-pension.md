# Partner pension (partnerpensioen)

## Who counts as a partner
A partner is your spouse (wife or husband), your registered partner, or the person you live with under a notarial cohabitation agreement who is registered with the fund. Cohabiting partners must be registered by the member through the member portal.

## Amount: what your partner receives if you die
When a member dies, the registered partner receives a partner pension of 70% of the member's retirement pension. If the member dies before retirement, the partner pension is based on the pension the member would have built up until state pension age.

## Orphan's pension
Children receive an orphan's pension of 14% of the retirement pension per child until the age of 25.
