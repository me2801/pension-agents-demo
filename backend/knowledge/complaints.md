# Complaints

## How to file a complaint
If you are not satisfied with our service, you can file a complaint through the member portal, by letter or by phone. Please describe what went wrong and what you would like us to do.

## Handling
Complaints are handled by a senior employee and are never answered automatically. You receive a confirmation within five working days and a decision within six weeks.

## If you disagree with the decision
You can ask the board of the fund to review the decision. After that, you can turn to the Pensions Ombudsman, free of charge.
