# Address changes

## Living in the Netherlands
The fund receives address changes automatically from the municipal personal records database (BRP). If you move within the Netherlands, you only need to register your new address with your municipality. You do not need to inform the fund.

## Living abroad
Members who live abroad are not registered in the BRP. They must report a change of address themselves through the member portal or by letter, including the new address and the date of the move. Please also report a change of bank account.

## Why your address matters
We use your address to send your annual pension overview and important letters. If we cannot reach you, payments may be delayed.
