# Changing jobs and value transfer (waardeoverdracht)

## When you change jobs or employer
When you leave your employer, you stop building up pension with the fund. The pension you have built up so far stays with the fund and is paid out from your state pension age.

## Value transfer
You can transfer the value of your accrued pension to the pension fund of your new employer. This is called value transfer. Your new pension fund requests the transfer on your behalf. The transfer is free of charge.

## Small pensions
Very small pensions (less than about 500 euros gross per year) are automatically transferred to your new pension fund.
