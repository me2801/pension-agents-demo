# Indexation (indexatie)

## What indexation means
Indexation means that pensions are increased to compensate for rising prices. The board decides every year in November whether pensions are increased on 1 January.

## Funding ratio (dekkingsgraad)
Indexation depends on the financial position of the fund, measured by the funding ratio (in Dutch: dekkingsgraad): the ratio between the fund's assets and its obligations. Full indexation is only possible when the funding ratio is high enough.

## No guarantee
Indexation is conditional: there is no guarantee that pensions will be increased in a given year.
